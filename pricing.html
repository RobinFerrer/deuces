Frequently Asked

Before you
sign up.

Can I try a trial period?

Yes. Ask reception for a free weekly pass — valid for one group class visit and one court booking. No obligation.

What's the minimum term?

Memberships run by calendar year. You can join mid-year and pay pro rata for the remaining months.

Is there a family discount?

From the third family member onwards you get a 20% discount per extra member. Request the package via contact.

Can I pause my membership?

Once per year you can pause your membership for 1–3 months (e.g. injury or travel). Send a message through the contact form.
