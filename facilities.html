What We Offer

All Under One Roof

Deuces features world-class facilities designed for comfort, performance, and community.

Indoor Courts

8 climate-controlled indoor courts with professional hardcourt surfaces, air conditioning, and full LED lighting — optimal conditions year-round.

Outdoor Courts

6 clay courts in the open air, surrounded by greenery. Equipped with floodlighting for evening play, and maintained to the highest standard.

Padel Courts

4 panoramic padel courts — 2 covered, 2 open-air. Glass walls throughout for optimal visibility and spectacle.

Fitness & Training

A fully equipped fitness centre with strength training, cardio, and functional zones — purpose-built for tennis and padel players.

Changing Rooms

Spacious, modern changing rooms for men and women. With private showers, lockers, and premium amenities.

Clubhouse & Lounge

An inviting lounge for before and after the match. Enjoy a drink, watch the courts, or unwind with your team in a warm atmosphere.

Pro Shop

Our pro shop stocks top equipment from the best brands. Including a professional stringing service and expert advice from certified specialists.

Video Analysis Suite

Our coaches use advanced video analysis software to evaluate your technique in detail. Every stroke measured, every movement optimised.

Parking

80 free parking spaces on site, including EV charging points. Bicycle parking is of course also provided.
