What is Padel?

Energy. Tactics.
Connection.

Padel combines elements of tennis and squash on an enclosed court. It's social, quick to learn, and intensely enjoyable for all ages and skill levels.

At Deuces we have 4 panoramic courts — 2 covered, 2 open-air — designed for the ultimate padel experience.

Why Padel?

4 reasons to
get started.

02
