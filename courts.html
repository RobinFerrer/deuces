Good to Know

Everything
you need to
get on court

Booking

Book online or at the front desk. Courts are open daily from 07:00 to 22:00. Same-day bookings welcome subject to availability.

Court Hire

Rackets and balls available to rent at reception. Coaching can be added to any booking. All equipment is cleaned and maintained between sessions.

Membership

Members enjoy priority booking, discounted rates, and full access to all 18 Deuces courts. Flexible monthly and annual plans available.

Dress Code

Non-marking soles required on all courts. Appropriate sportswear expected at all times. Changing rooms and showers available at the facility.
