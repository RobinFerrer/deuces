The Program

For players who
aim higher.

The Pro Program at Deuces is designed for competitive players who want to elevate their game to a professional level. Intensive daily training, video analysis, and mental coaching.

Get in Touch

Method

Built for the
long game.

Pro isn't a sprint. Every player gets a 12-month development plan built from technical pillars, physical periodisation and tournament targets. We measure, we adapt, we repeat.

  • Individual 12-month development plan
  • Monthly benchmarks and KPIs
  • Full access to the Deuces fitness centre
  • Sports psychologist on call
Meet the Pro coaches

Tournament Circuit

On the road
with your coach.

Pro Program players get on-site tournament support: technical warm-up, in-match coaching between sets, and post-match video analysis. No tournament alone.

  • Coaching at national and ITF tournaments
  • Travel planning, logistics and hotel bookings
  • Integrated tournament debriefs
