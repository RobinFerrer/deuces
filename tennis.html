Our Game

Tennis is more
than a sport.

At Deuces we believe tennis builds character. Discipline, patience and passion — on every court, at every level. Our academy is built around the player: every program begins with listening, observing and a game plan tailored to you.

Whether you pick up a racket today or play a tournament tomorrow — we build your game with the same Deuces standard.

Outdoor — Clay

Six courts,
one classic.

Our six outdoor courts are maintained daily to French clay protocol. A slower game, longer rallies, and the technique-demanding surface that makes every player better.

  • 6 courts, daily swept and lined
  • Open March through November
  • LED lighting for evening play
  • Tournaments and ladder competitions
