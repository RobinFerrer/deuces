Our Story

Play.

Deuces is more than an academy — it is a community of players who believe the game begins with the right atmosphere. Our indoor and outdoor courts are designed for both the beginner and the seasoned competitor.

From a powerful serve to a delicate slice — at Deuces, every stroke is guided by experienced coaches who breathe passion for the game.
