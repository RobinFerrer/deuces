Frequently Asked

A quick
check first.

Do I need to be a member to book a court?

No. Non-members can book day-to-day through the online tool. Members get access up to 14 days ahead and a 30% lower rate.

Do you have rackets to rent?

Yes — tennis and padel rackets are available at reception for €5 per session. Padel balls are included in your court rental.

How does a trial lesson work?

A trial lesson is 30 minutes and free for new members. Our coach assesses your level and suggests a suitable program. No commitment.

Is there parking?

Yes — 60 free spaces, including 4 EV chargers and 6 covered bike racks.

Can I book a private event?

Absolutely. We regularly host clinics, corporate days and birthdays. Use the form above for a tailored quote.
