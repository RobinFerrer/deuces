Our Values

2015 — Founding

It began with
two courts.

Deuces began in 2015 from a simple observation: the region had courts enough, but too few places where the game was respected. We started with two gravel courts, a converted container for changing rooms, and the conviction that atmosphere matters as much as technique.

2019 — Expansion

A roof over
the game.

Four years later: eight covered hardcourt courts, our own clubhouse with bar, and the region's first padel platform. Deuces became a place where the game was played year-round, regardless of weather.

2025 — Today

An academy,
a community.

Today Deuces guides over 600 active members, twelve coaches run daily programs, and our Pro Program is recognised within the Belgian top tier. But the foundation is unchanged: a place that breathes the game.
